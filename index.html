🌸 PCOD & PCOS Exercises

A daily flow designed to balance hormones.

  • 🧘‍♀️ Butterfly Pose (2 mins)

    Bring soles of feet together, knees out. Flap knees gently like butterfly wings.

  • 🧘‍♀️ Cobra Pose (1 min)

    Lie on your belly, press your hands into the floor, and gently lift your chest upwards.

  • 🧘‍♀️ Cat-Cow Stretch (10 reps)

    From all fours, arch your back up like a cat, then drop your belly down like a cow.

  • 🧘‍♀️ Garland Pose (1 min)

    Squat down low with your feet wide out, bringing your hands gracefully to your heart.

  • 🧘‍♀️ Bridge Pose (1 min)

    Lie on your back, knees bent. Lift your hips toward the ceiling, creating a bridge shape.

  • 🧘‍♀️ Child's Pose (2 mins)

    Kneel, sit on your heels, and fold forward, resting your forehead on the floor for deep relaxation.

  • 🧘‍♀️ Pigeon Pose (1 min per side)

    Stretch your hip and lower back by bringing one knee forward and extending the other leg back.

  • 🧘‍♀️ Reclining Bound Angle (2 mins)

    Lie down with soles of feet together and knees falling open. Place one hand on heart, one on belly.
